Jiyang Lee

I'm a Ph.D. candidate at Seoul National University, where I work on robot learning with Prof. Seong-Woo Kim (Civil and Environmental Engineering). From January to July 2026, I was an international visiting graduate student at the University of Waterloo with Prof. Roya Firoozi, and our collaboration continues. I received my Master's degree and Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Sogang University in 2017 and 2015, respectively. Previously, I worked as a research engineer at LG Display (2020-2024), and as a robotics researcher at KIST (2018-2020).

Research

I work on robot learning. I fine-tune and evaluate π0.5, Diffusion Policy, and OpenVLA in simulation and on real robot arms, and build end-to-end pipelines for demonstration collection, policy training, and closed-loop deployment. I have also started a humanoid (Unitree G1) project, building the data-generation and whole-body tracking infrastructure needed to study when world-action-model predictions remain executable.
