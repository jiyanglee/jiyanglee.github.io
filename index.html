Jiyang Lee

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Civil & Environment Engineering at Seoul National University, where I'm advised by Prof. Seong-Woo Kim. I'm currently an international visiting graduate student at the University of Waterloo (2026), where I'm advised by Prof. Roya Firoozi. I received my Master's degree and Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Sogang University in 2017 and 2015, respectively. Previously, I worked as a research engineer at LG Display (2020-2024), and as a robotics researcher at KIST (2018-2020).
