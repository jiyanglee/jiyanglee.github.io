Conference Deadlines

Open submission deadlines for robotics, ML, CV, and control conferences. Entries drop off the list once their deadline passes. D-day is counted from today.

Plain dates are published by the organisers. ~italic dates are projected from the previous edition because no CFP is out yet — treat them as rough, and check the linked site before planning around one.

This is a personal list kept by hand, not an official source. Dates can be extended, moved, or mistyped, and time zones (AoE, PT, UTC) differ between venues. Always confirm the deadline, time zone, and submission rules on the official call for papers before you rely on any date here.
