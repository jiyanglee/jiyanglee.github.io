Jiyang Lee is a Ph.D. student in Civil, Urban and Environmental Engineering at Seoul National University, where he is advised by Prof. Seong-Woo Kim and works on robotic learning, embodied AI, and vision-language-action models. He is currently an international visiting graduate student at the University of Waterloo (2026), where he is advised by Prof. Roya Firoozi. He received a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Sogang University in 2017, where he was advised by Prof. Cheol-soo Lee, and a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Sogang University in 2015. Previously, he worked as a research engineer at LG Display (2020-2024), where he developed few-shot learning classification models for OLED defect images and built AI-based anomaly detection systems. He also worked as a robotics researcher at KIST (2018-2020), where he designed quadruped robots and developed panoramic view systems for firefighting robots. He received the Best Paper Award from the Korean Society of Manufacturing Technology Engineers in 2017, the Robot Membership Award from Samsung Techwin in 2014, and the Excellence Award from the TI Innovation Challenge: Korean MCU Design Contest in 2013.
